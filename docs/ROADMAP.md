# Roadmap

## Delivered foundation

- Valid custom integration, config and options flows, versioned persistence
- Sensorless plants, care profiles, manual watering, durable care history
- Fixed schedule and next-check calculation
- Home Assistant entities and actions
- Independent multi-measurement assignments, reassignment, moisture-aware care,
  availability handling, and schedule fallback
- Optional existing-plant and OpenPlantbook references

## Future: plant knowledge

Explicit reference-data retrieval, cached defaults, and richer environment
ranges. User overrides will continue to win.

## Future: smart care

Conservative temperature, humidity, season, rainfall, and weather influence.

## Future: full care

Pruning, custom activities, notes, photos, growth tracking, vacation mode,
household assignments, labels, voice improvements, diagnosis, and learned
watering intervals.
